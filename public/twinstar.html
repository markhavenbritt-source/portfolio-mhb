Twin Star Branding

PROBLEM: Comics brands lean nostalgic. Needed to feel current across a wide range of stories.

SOLUTION: Built a sharp, modern identity designed to stand apart, not blend in.

ROLE: Creative Director

How We Did It

Defined a clear point of view early

Set the tone around confidence, clarity, and cultural relevance. No retro, no irony.

Built a system, not just a logo

Developed type, color, and structure that could scale across platform, product, and marketing.

Designed for where it's going

Made decisions with future product, content, and growth in mind—not just launch.

Impact

Established a distinct brand in a crowded, nostalgia-heavy category

Created a system ready to scale across product and content

Set a clear creative foundation for future creators and collaborators
