Frequently asked questions

What is MemoryBox?

MemoryBox is a private AI memory and context workspace. It helps you keep the conversations, files, ideas, preferences, and project information you choose in one place, so you can bring better context to supported AI tools.

How is MemoryBox different from an AI chatbot?

A chatbot is where you have an AI conversation. MemoryBox is where you keep the context that makes those conversations useful. It helps you organize information across projects and bring the right background into your work with AI.

Can I use MemoryBox for separate projects or areas of life?

Yes. Create separate Memory Spaces for projects, clients, research, work, or personal life. This helps keep relevant context available without blending unrelated information together.

Is MemoryBox private?

MemoryBox is built around a private vault and user control. You choose what to add, what to use, and what to remove. Visit the Privacy & Data page for clear details about workspace data and AI requests.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. MemoryBox is designed for people who want more useful AI without managing complicated prompts or rebuilding context manually. Organize information around your projects and priorities—not technical settings.

Which AI tools does MemoryBox work with?

MemoryBox works alongside the AI tools you already use. The MemoryBox browser extension adds your memory to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Doubao, and MemoryBox can automatically sync your Claude Code and Codex sessions. You can also import your past ChatGPT conversations. Supported tools are always growing; see the MemoryBox app for the latest list.

Can I delete information from MemoryBox?

Yes, and you're always in control. Delete a single memory or document with the trash icon, or ask MemoryBox in chat to forget everything about a topic; it will confirm before deleting anything. To clear data in bulk, go to Settings → Privacy & Reset and choose what to remove, such as memories, chat history, documents, or connected accounts. Deletions are permanent.

Is MemoryBox like Dropbox? Does it work with my cloud storage?

MemoryBox isn't cloud storage; it works with the storage you already use. Connect Dropbox, Box, Google Drive, OneDrive, or SharePoint, choose the files you want, and MemoryBox turns them into memory your AI tools can use. Your original files stay where they are.
