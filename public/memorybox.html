MemoryBox Brand

PROBLEM: Deep-tech company. First consumer product. No brand to launch it with.

SOLUTION: Built the identity, landing page, and brand guide for the beta launch, then carried it across social, video, and events.

ROLE: Fractional Creative Director

How We Did It

Started with one idea

"You are your memory." Every visual decision had to serve that line: you at the center, AI in orbit around you.

Three directions, one decision

Mood boards with the stakeholders, three creative directions, one pick. No agency entourage, no endless rounds.

Built it to be handed off

Wrote a brand guide plain enough for in-house designers and freelancers to use without me in the room.

Impact

Took an infrastructure company's first consumer product to public beta with a brand in place

One system across the landing page, social, sizzle reel, and event swag

A brand guide the team can run with as the company scales
